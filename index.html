12 Battle-Tested Modules Ready to Deploy for Your Business

Every module below is already running under live call-center and warehouse loads—ready to be tailored to your domain.

My Queue & 5-Min Cart Capture

02

Customer 360° One View

Single-screen workspace showing Open Orders (preventing duplicate orders), Online + Offline Store ERP bills, visual red-marked item returns, Credit Note ledgers, Referral Wallets, and Call History.

03

Session-Level Agent Forensics

S3-archived session tracking for every agent login. Supervisors can inspect session duration, IP address, every Page Visited, exact Search Queries typed, and every Order/Cart Change made.

04

3-Tier Granular RBAC Engine

Zero-code access control: 1) Create Permissions with granular View, Add, Update, and Delete toggles per module, 2) Assign Permissions to Roles, and 3) Assign Roles to Users.

05

Live System Settings Engine

Customize Free Delivery Thresholds, Delivery/Handling Fees, Wallet Min/Max Order % Caps, Lead Queue Timers, Session Presence, and Background Jobs—taking effect across the platform in 60 seconds.

06

Live Supervisor Telemetry

Track First Login, Last Logout, frequent Login/Logout toggling ratios, Agent Idle Time (no leads received/processed), and Lead Aging Time against SLA benchmarks in real time.

07

Priority Waterfall & Instant Callback

Route leads across Unassigned, To Be Assigned (auto-recovered when an agent logs out mid-lead), Follow-Up, Cancelled Recovery Queue, and urgent customer-triggered Instant Callbacks.

08

ERP Transition Failure Recovery

Never lose an order to an ERP timeout or unmapped SKU again. Failed transitions are isolated with exact ERP error logs and one-click single or bulk Re-sync to ERP recovery.

09

Smart Substitute & Upsell Engine

Auto-suggests higher-margin private-label substitutes, bulk quantity tier discounts (e.g., 3 units for +2% off), 1-click full past order reorders, and 30/60/90-day subscription reminders.

10

Multi-Branch Slot & Cutoff Engine

Validates live branch-wise stock and daily cutoff times (e.g., 12 PM cutoff for Same-Day 12–5 PM / 5–11 PM slots, or 7-day forward scheduling) and excludes Store Pickups from fleet routing.

11

5-Layer Order & Cart Audit Logs

Every order maintains immutable Order Logs, Slot Logs, Cart Logs (tracking whether the Customer or Agent added/modified quantities), Live ERP Stock Status, and Queue Logs.

Dedicated Support Dashboard with 2h/4h/24h/48h SLA breach alerts (1-hour warning), post-warehouse customer self-service ticket creation, and 1-click agent ticket escalation inside Order Details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ERP integration pre-built, or do you integrate with whatever ERP we use?

We custom-integrate directly with whatever ERP your business runs (SAP, Tally Prime, Microsoft Dynamics, Oracle, Medica, or custom SQL/REST APIs). And because ERP networks occasionally experience downtime or unmapped item codes, our built-in Order Transition Failed queue captures any rejected order so your team can re-sync it in one click.

How does Click-to-Call telephony integration work?

Our CRM supports third-party Click-to-Call cloud telephony integration. We can integrate with your existing telephony vendor or set you up quickly through our partnered telephony provider—enabling 1-click outbound dialing, automatic customer lookup on incoming calls, and automated call status disposition logs.

Can supervisors see exact agent searches and changes inside each login session?

Yes. Every agent session is logged and archived to S3 with full forensic detail—showing session duration, IP address, every URL/page visited, exact product or customer search strings typed by the agent, and every cart or order status modification.
